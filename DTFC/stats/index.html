Season record

How this is calculated

Points are 3 for a win, 1 for a draw, 0 for a loss. Pts/Game is points divided by appearances — a fairer ranking than raw points, since players turn out a different number of times. Streak is the best run of wins this season across the games that player actually appeared in — weeks they missed are skipped rather than breaking the run, and a draw ends it. An asterisk means the run is still live, like a batsman not out. GD is the goal difference of the team the player was on. Everything here covers season 26-27 only; earlier seasons were never recorded match by match. Click any heading to sort.

Whites v Oranges

How this is calculated

Teams are re-picked every week, so this tracks the shirts rather than any settled group of players. Points are 3 for a win, 1 for a draw. Games without a recorded score are left out.

Captains

How this is calculated

Whoever picked the teams that week. A good captain picks a close game, so this measures the margin — which side they ended up on is beside the point. GD is the total winning margin across their games and Goals is every goal scored in them. GD/Game is the one that matters: the lower it is, the more even their teams were. Ranked best first; the worst tag marks the highest average margin. Every game they picked counts, whether or not they played.

Best combinations

How this is calculated

Win rate for players who lined up on the same team, minimum 5 games together, top 5 in each size. A draw is not a win. Ties break on games played, so a pairing from more games outranks one from fewer.

Career appearances

How this is calculated

Top 15 by total appearances, counted from the line-up of every match on record — 237 games back to September 2020.

Games per season

Turnout

How this is calculated

Share of games a player turned out for. Click any column heading to re-order. All time is their appearances as a share of every game the club has played, so it rewards longevity as much as attendance — anyone who joined recently sits low by definition. Since debut fixes that: it only counts games played after they first turned out, so it measures how often someone shows up rather than how long they have been around. Exact counts are in the all players table.

Dropouts

How this is calculated

You were down to play that week and then didn’t. Swapping or giving your spot away counts — the test is whether you played, not how you got out of it. Availability comes from both WhatsApp groups; whether you played comes from the line-up, which reconciles exactly with the wiki. It misses anyone put on a posted team sheet who never typed “in”. The chat export stops in July 2026, so from 26-27 onward dropouts are recorded by hand each week instead — and since anyone down to play either played or dropped, commitments for those seasons are appearances plus dropouts.
